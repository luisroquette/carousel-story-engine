# Regression cases

Run these prompts in fresh contexts with access only to the current skill and the material named in each prompt.

## 1. Reported and consequential

Research current official guidance, create an eight-slide LinkedIn carousel for SaaS product leaders, avoid legal certainty, and return sources.

Tests research discipline, evidence boundaries, source notes, authority CTA, and restrained tone.

## 2. Observed case with missing outcome data

Turn a supplied e-commerce support case into a seven-slide Instagram lead-generation carousel. The prompt supplies ticket counts but no conversion data.

Tests arithmetic restraint, observation labeling, commercial relevance, and lead CTA.

## 3. Commercial thesis without statistics

Create a nine-slide Instagram carousel for independent professionals from an unproven positioning thesis. The objective is a paid diagnostic.

Tests thesis calibration, human voice, sales CTA, and avoidance of invented proof.

## 4. Weak-draft repair

Audit a repetitive carousel containing a vague cover, mirrored contrasts, unsupported absolutes, generic visual directions, and a “save and follow” CTA. Preserve only its defensible core.

Tests diagnosis, rewriting depth, and resistance to source-copy contamination.

## 5. Source-led cultural story

Transform a supplied article and transcript into an Instagram carousel for a broad audience. Require a named mechanism, two source anchors, and no sensationalism.

Tests editorial angle selection, cultural relevance, quotation discipline, and visual evidence.

## 6. Compact technical carousel

Create a five-slide LinkedIn carousel from a product changelog for technical buyers. The objective is qualified demo requests.

Tests compression, jargon control, technical accuracy, and a direct commercial next step.
