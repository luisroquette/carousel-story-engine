# Carousel output quality rubric

Use this rubric for regression testing. Score each dimension from 1 to 10.

## Dimensions

1. **Truth and evidence**: factual claims are traceable; observations and illustrative examples are labeled; theses stay calibrated.
2. **Hook contract**: the cover is specific, credible, distinctive, and fully repaid by the deck.
3. **Narrative progression**: every slide adds a fact, mechanism, consequence, example, decision, or useful reframe.
4. **Specificity and density**: concrete nouns, actors, actions, artifacts, and mechanisms replace generic advice.
5. **Natural voice**: the copy sounds native to its language and audience, without AI clichés, translated-English compression, or mechanical rhythm.
6. **Audience payoff**: the reader can see why the topic matters to their situation and what becomes clearer after reading.
7. **Practical value**: the deck gives a usable diagnosis, decision, question, model, or action without pretending certainty.
8. **Objective and CTA fit**: the final action follows naturally from the value delivered and matches reach, authority, leads, or sales.
9. **Visual executability**: every direction specifies an appropriate component and composition; layouts vary for informational reasons.
10. **Delivery hygiene**: publishable copy is separated from production notes; captions add new value; the deck remains legible on mobile.

## Critical failures

Any critical failure rejects the output regardless of its average score:

- invented fact, statistic, quotation, source, or result;
- a thesis or illustrative example presented as measured evidence;
- legal, medical, or financial certainty beyond the sources;
- a cover promise that the deck does not fulfill;
- repeated slides that only paraphrase the premise;
- generic engagement CTA unrelated to the delivered value;
- visual directions a designer cannot execute without inventing the layout;
- visible internal scoring, rejected drafts, or chain-of-thought inside publishable copy;
- copied wording or visual identity from a reference creator.

## Passing standard

- no critical failures;
- every dimension scores at least 8;
- average score is at least 9;
- no banned AI cliché or artificial suspense;
- a second independent run must pass the same case after any corrective revision.

For ordinary one-off generation, rerun the complete scoring pass after a correction. The fresh independent rerun is the release standard for regression tests and distributed skill revisions.
